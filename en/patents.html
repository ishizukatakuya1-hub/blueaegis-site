JP Patent No. 7946311

System, method and program for deriving the executable set of identity verification methods and identifying the undetermined-alternative-route class

Patent numberJP 7946311
ApplicationJP 2026-187663, filed 12 August 2026
ExaminationRequested 17 September 2026 (accelerated examination)
Registered29 October 2026
Gazette13 November 2026
Claims9
IPCG06Q 50/26, G06Q 50/10

The problem it addresses

Under Article 4 of Japan’s Act on Prevention of Transfer of Criminal Proceeds, obliged businesses must verify customers at the time of a transaction. The permitted methods are exhaustively enumerated in Article 6 of the enforcement regulation, and a method outside that list cannot be used to fill a gap.

That list is being rewritten. An amending order promulgated on 24 June 2025 and effective 1 April 2027 abolishes the non-face-to-face method that combines an image of a photo identity document with an image of the person’s face, on the ground that forged or altered documents make it too easy to impersonate someone. The remaining methods shift away from document images and towards reading the data recorded on an IC chip.

As a result, there will structurally be people for whom none of the enumerated methods can be completed: those without a public electronic credential, those whose device cannot read one, those whose certificate has expired, those who cannot supply the PIN it requires. Whether any method survives for a given person, once several of these apply at once, cannot be known without checking that person’s situation against the requirements of each method.

Conventional verification systems present the list of methods, let the person choose, and switch to another method when one fails partway. Under that design, the fact that no method can be completed only emerges after a sequence of failures. Where the rules do not allow a failed attempt to be supplemented, that late discovery means repeated procedures, tangled verification records, customers abandoning the process, and transactions left in limbo.

And because the list itself is amended repeatedly, unless you can later establish which version of the rules a given determination was made under, you cannot demonstrate that it was correct to an auditor or a regulator.

What the patent covers

Claim 1, in outline: the system obtains information about the person’s attributes and about which verification means are available to them (documents, electronic credentials, device capabilities, authentication information). Before verification is started, it checks these against the information defining each method’s requirements, and derives the set of methods that can be completed for that person.

Where that set is empty, or where the attribute or means information is not settled enough to complete the check, the person is placed in the undetermined-alternative-route class. The system also retains a record of the basis for the determination — the version identifier used, paired with the items that were checked — in a form that allows the identity of that basis to be verified afterwards, and outputs it on request from an external system.

The dependent claims cover re-running the derivation for existing subjects when a new version of the rules is received and updating the class accordingly; outputting the cause of the classification and which items remain unsettled; generating control information that holds verification back from starting; ordering the presentation or execution of methods by expected completion, time and cost when more than one remains; and applying a hash, an electronic signature or a timestamp to the record of the basis so that tampering can be detected.

Where it applies

  • Pre-flight determination for obliged businesses under the Act — banks and other financial institutions, crypto asset exchange providers, mobile carriers, second-hand dealers, real estate businesses
  • Working out, ahead of the 1 April 2027 effective date, where the current verification flow breaks and which customer segments can no longer be verified
  • Showing an auditor or a regulator which version of the rules a determination was made under, and which items were examined
  • Re-running past determinations against a new version of the rules when they are amended, and updating the classification
