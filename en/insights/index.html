REGULATORY INSIGHTS

Insights

Notes on regulations coming into force and being amended — what operators are required to do, and what they should be recording. We monitor the relevant instruments and standards daily, and write when something changes that affects practice. Each note reflects publicly available material and is not legal advice.
More notes are available in Japanese: 規制解説.

  • 22 October 2026

    EU Cyber Resilience Act reporting — records that let you show you met the 24-hour, 72-hour and 14-day deadlines

    Manufacturers placing products on the EU market now have to report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents. The clock starts at awareness — which means awareness has to be recorded.
  • 22 October 2026

    Breach reporting under Japan's 2026 APPI amendments — wider exceptions to notifying individuals, and keeping the basis for not reporting

    The amending Act passed in July 2026 widens the exceptions to notifying individuals and allows lighter reporting under rules yet to be written. A decision not to report needs a record behind it.
  • 22 October 2026

    Japan's Act on Strengthening Cyber Response Capabilities — what critical infrastructure operators must notify and report from 1 October 2026

    The notification and reporting duties under Japan's active cyber defence legislation began on 1 October 2026. Deadlines for registering critical computers, first and detailed reports, and the records that support them.
  • 22 October 2026

    Transaction records under Japan's revised Subcontract Act (Toriteki Act) — the 18 items of Article 7, and records that show price talks took place

    On 1 January 2026 Japan's Subcontract Act became the Toriteki Act, and recommendations under it have begun. The record-keeping duty, and the new ban on setting prices without consultation.
  • 22 October 2026

    Beneficial shareholder identification and virtual-only meetings under Japan's Companies Act review — the records listed companies would keep

    A Legislative Council subcommittee is moving to final proposals on beneficial shareholder identification and virtual-only meetings. The points in the draft that turn on records and proof — for custodians and overseas investors too.
  • 22 August 2026

    Crypto-assets move under Japan’s FIEA — insider trading rules are coming

    The centre of gravity moves from the Payment Services Act to the FIEA, and insider trading rules arrive. Why sources of information that are spread out make the regulated position hard to pin down.
  • 22 August 2026

    Software as a medical device — the difficulty of not exceeding the approved scope

    Updates, sales copy and wider integration move the scope without anyone intending it. Two-stage approval sets that scope deliberately narrow to begin with.
  • 22 August 2026

    The EU Digital Identity Wallet — relying party registration begins on 24 December 2026

    Registration, the duty to stay within the purpose you declared, and registers that will not be built alike from one Member State to the next.
  • 22 August 2026

    Japan's non-face-to-face identity verification changes on 1 April 2027

    The photo-ID-image method is retired in favour of IC chip reading. Three amending orders overlap, there are no transitional measures, and one widely repeated requirement does not appear in the primary sources.
  • 22 August 2026

    The EU AI Act transparency obligations — what became enforceable on 2 August 2026

    Disclosure for interactive AI, deepfake labelling, machine-readable marking. The extraterritorial basis for reaching operators outside the EU, and why labelling alone is not the hard part.
